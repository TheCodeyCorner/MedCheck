Medicine Authenticity Check

If the details on your medicine match all the details shown on a medicine card—including the medicine name, manufacturer, active ingredient, medicine type, strength, quantity, and other available identifying information—the medicine is consistent with an authentic record in our database.

If your medicine does not match any available medicine card, be cautious. This does not necessarily mean the medicine is counterfeit, as the product may be missing from our database or the information may have changed.

In case of no match, verify the medicine using official manufacturer information, packaging details, batch/lot numbers, expiry dates, authorized pharmacies, or other trusted sources.

Important: A database match alone cannot guarantee that a physical medicine is genuine. Always verify the product through appropriate official or professional sources when authenticity is in doubt.
